Meet Your Guide

Taught by an educator who's done this for years — just not under this name yet.

Kimberly Stewart is a STEM Teacher at Groton Middle School and an Adjunct Professor at Quinnipiac University, where she trains future educators in technology integration. She's spent her career building coding and robotics programs that get kids genuinely excited — not because it's required, but because the lesson connects to something they actually care about.

CodeTrek Robotics grew out of a simple observation: coding and robotics still aren't available in many Connecticut schools. CodeTrek brings that opportunity directly to after-school programs, recreation centers, and homeschool groups — starting with coding, with robotics kits joining the lineup soon.
